# 不要用新闻叙事为交易亏损找借口

[原帖](https://x.com/TradercBTC/status/2096927320502362125) · 2026-09-07 19:43（北京时间）

## 原帖内容

The market, or retail traders in particular, crave a plausible explanation for market movements, even though such reasons have no bearing on price swings or their own trading decisions. That is why there are countless financial analysts and news commentators in the market — an industry born to cater to this demand. Their job is to come up with rational justifications: for instance, asset X rose or fell because of event Y. Retail traders then use these narratives to rationalize their losses. Take an example: Xiao Ming went long on gold, yet gold prices dropped. He turns to news and media reports and convinces himself that gold fell because of such‑and‑such an event. Even if the news is irrelevant to his loss and trading choices, he clings to that explanation to justify his losing trade. He tells himself it was not his poor decision‑making, but the occurrence of that external event that drove prices down, as a way to seek psychological comfort.

## 中文理解（整理者翻译）

作者认为，散户常渴望为市场波动寻找看似合理的解释，市场评论行业迎合这种需要；但这些解释可能与自己的具体交易决策及亏损原因无关。文中以小明做多黄金后下跌为例：他借助新闻把亏损归因于外部事件，藉此获得安慰，却可能绕开对自身决策的检讨。

**这里批评的是用事后叙事替代复盘；不能据此推出新闻事件永远不会影响价格。**
